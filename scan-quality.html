How to read this

Clean rate — of the scans a driver added to their route, the share that needed no corrections at the review step. This is the headline accuracy number. Capital letters and spacing don't count as corrections; a different word or number does.

Discarded — the driver threw the reading away. Couldn't read — no address could be found on the photo. Errors — the reading service failed. Waiting — read, but the driver hasn't answered yet (or left the page).

Tokens — the amount of text/image the AI processed for each scan: this is what you are charged for. Multiply by your model's price on Anthropic's pricing page to compare the cost of models. Avg time is the average, so one slow scan can pull it up.

Groups with fewer than 20 confirmed scans are shown in grey: too few to draw conclusions from.
