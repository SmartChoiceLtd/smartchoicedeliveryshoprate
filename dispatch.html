Offer a job

Pick a shop or type the pickup address. Add how many deliveries and the area if you like, and the text to the driver is written for you. If a driver would collect from 2 or 3 shops, add each one. Drivers are ranked by driving time to the first pickup, from where they will be free: the address they said they are heading to, otherwise their last delivery. Drivers with an old update are listed after current ones.

Where drivers are

Updates arrive automatically when a driver submits a delivery or taps Heading to on their route page. Refreshes every 30 seconds.
