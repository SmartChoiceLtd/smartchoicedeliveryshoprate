Christmas greens …

Drivers enter how many of a delivery’s pieces are Christmas greens. Each green piece adds to the driver’s pay and to the shop’s charge, on top of the usual rate and extra-piece amount. It applies only to deliveries dated November 1 to January 31, and only while this switch is on.

A change applies to deliveries entered from now on. Deliveries already entered keep the rates they were entered with.
