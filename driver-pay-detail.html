Driver Pay Detail

Pay Ledger← Hub
Shows what is recorded in the pay ledger: each pay statement emailed, e-transfers, advances, other payments and adjustments. Pay is the driver's current pay for the week, worked out from the orders as they stand now (the same figure as the Pay Ledger page). Statement emailed is what the driver was sent; a warning shows when the two differ. Still owing = current pay + adjustments - paid. Voided entries are kept for the audit trail, struck through, and are not counted. When a statement was emailed more than once for a week, the latest one is shown.
